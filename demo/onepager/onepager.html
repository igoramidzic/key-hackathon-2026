The problem

Critical outages stay down longer than they need to

When a critical application fails, fixing it is only part of the job. Someone has to notice, pull the right people together, hunt for the root cause, ship the fix and keep ServiceNow up to date. All of that takes time, and every hour an application is down costs the bank revenue and customers.

1 hour 18 minutesAverage time to resolve a P1 today
3 hours 23 minutesAverage time to resolve a P2 today
The solution

NightWatch runs the incident from the first error to the last ticket

01Detect

Watches production logs, alerts and new ServiceNow incidents around the clock, and spots trouble within seconds.

02Report

Opens the incident and runs KeyBank's Critical Incident Management process, ServiceNow records included.

03Assemble

Sets up the Teams channel and call, and brings in the right support teams and engineers.

04Remediate

Traces the failure to the change that caused it, explains it in plain words, and proposes a fix for an engineer to approve. Then it closes out the paperwork.
